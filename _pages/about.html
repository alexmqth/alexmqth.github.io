About me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Lequan Yu.

My research focuses on multimodal learning and efficient vision-language models, with applications in medical AI. I study how models combine incomplete evidence, retain useful visual information, and reason efficiently.

Previously, I received my MS from UESTC, advised by Prof. Xiaoshuang Shi, and my BS from Harbin Engineering University. I also worked with Prof. Huan Song at West China Hospital.

Research experience

The University of Hong Kong

09/2025 – 08/2026

Research Assistant · Advisor: Lequan Yu

Developed PRISM for efficient 3D medical vision-language classification. Spatio-anatomical evidence scoring and foveated token routing retain diagnostic regions while using only 15.6%–18.8% of the original visual tokens, evaluated on bone tumor CT and cardiac MRI.

West China Hospital

08/2024 – 08/2025

Research Intern · Biomedical Big Data Center · Advisor: Huan Song

Developed AURA for cross-center medical prediction with missing modalities at inference, using epistemic uncertainty for routing and calibration. Validated on five datasets across the US, China, and Korea.
